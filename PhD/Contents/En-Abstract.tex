\chapter*{\centerline{Abstract}}
\label{EN-Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\begin{latin}
An initial-value problem for a dynamical system is formulated, and sufficient conditions for existence and uniqueness are stated. The explicit Euler approximation is then introduced, and its error is compared for several time-step values. The example presents a compact structure for a mathematical problem, method, and result.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\textbf{Keywords:}
dynamical system, initial-value problem, stability, numerical convergence.
\end{latin}
